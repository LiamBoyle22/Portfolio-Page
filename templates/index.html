About

A little about me.

I'm Liam Boyle, a Master's student in Computer Science at Sacred Heart University, working toward a career in software engineering with a focus on machine learning and computer vision. My path here started in Immersive Media & Mixed Reality, where I first got interested in how machines interpret and act on visual information, a curiosity that's carried directly into my current graduate studies. Since then, I've built a foundation in languages like Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, and many frameworks to support these projects, along with cloud infrastructure on AWS and Azure. That breadth matters to me, I don't just want to train models, I want to understand and own the systems that put them into production. Right now, my focus is narrowing deliberately. I'm drawn to computer vision and applied ML to gain a better understanding of what's happening under the hood, and building the infrastructure that lets these systems that we count on so much, run reliably in the real world. I'm currently based in the NYC/Connecticut area, and actively looking for opportunities to grow as an ML/CV-focused software engineer. When I'm not at class, I'm usually programming, out triathalon training, or studying.
